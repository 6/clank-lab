# Edition reference map

For those historical references, use the same sections and appendices in
checks/checking-notes.pdf, including the old Appendix A (the twelve
formal hypotheses). Statement locations are translated below.
The notes keep every original full proof and all original unnumbered
mathematical prose, conventions and dependencies. Most certificate statements are now also in the article; the five
remaining notes statements and all original full proofs retain their
historical locations.
