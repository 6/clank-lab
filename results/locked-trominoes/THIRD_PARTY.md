# Third-party material

No third-party material is included in this folder. The block data, the
checkers, the Lean sources, the figures and the paper were produced for
this result; no figure or code from Tucker-Foltz's paper or its software is
copied.

The Lean project depends only on [Lean 4](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4)
v4.34.0 and its bundled standard library, distributed upstream under
Apache-2.0 ([Lean license](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/blob/v4.34.0/LICENSE)).
It is installed by elan, not included here.

The optional search program in `checks/search/` imports
[python-sat](https://github.com/pysathq/pysat) (MIT license), which the
reader installs separately; it is not included and is not needed for the
check.
