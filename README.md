# Clank Lab

Proposed solutions to open problems in math.

Not yet reviewed by experts. Corrections are welcome as pull requests or issues.

The proofs, formalizations, programs and text were produced by AI models working under the author's direction.

## Theorems

Results that settle a conjecture or question in general.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [Uniform 5-polytopes](results/uniform-5-polytopes/) | Whether the known convex [uniform 5-polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_5-polytope) (those built by Wythoff's construction, prisms and products) are all of them. Four dimensions was settled by Conway and Guy in 1965 (proved complete by Möller in 2004); five dimensions was listed as open by Schulte (2017) and Winter (2021). We give a computer-assisted proof that the known list is complete. | October 20, 2026 |
| [The EKG sequence](results/ekg-sequence/) | The second-order growth of the [EKG sequence](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/EKGSequence.html), conjectured by Lagarias, Rains and Sloane in 2002. Hofman and Pilipczuk proved a weaker ratio form, and Tomer Haver the upper half of the bound in 2026. We prove the lower half, completing the proof, and settle OEIS conjectures that follow. | October 20, 2026 |
| [A planar graph without 4- or 9-cycles](results/steinberg-c4-c9/) | Whether every planar graph with no 4- or 9-cycles is [3-colorable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%B6tzsch%27s_theorem), asked by Lu and coauthors in 2009. We give a computer-assisted proof that one isn't: an 82-vertex counterexample. | October 20, 2026 |
| [Stanley's conjecture on Smith forms of Specht modules](results/stanley-smith-forms/) | Whether the Smith normal form of each [Jucys–Murphy element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jucys%E2%80%93Murphy_element) acting on an integral Specht module is given by products of contents, as conjectured by Richard Stanley in 2016. We give a computer-assisted proof that it is not: the smallest counterexamples have shape (3,1,1). | October 21, 2026 |
| [The Smith form of the n-cycle class sum](results/stanley-class-sum-snf/) | The [Smith normal form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_normal_form) of multiplication by the sum of all $n$-cycles in the center of $\mathbb{Z}S_n$, asked by Richard Stanley in [2019](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/335226). We give the invariant factors for every $n$, proving his two conjectured special cases. | October 21, 2026 |

## Computed values

Results that settle one specific open case, such as a value a published survey lists as unknown.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [The seventh density Hales–Jewett number](results/density-seven/) | The largest set of seven-letter words over three letters containing no combinatorial line (the seventh [density Hales–Jewett number](https://oeis.org/A156989)). Exact values were known up to six letters. For seven, D.H.J. Polymath found 1,302 words in 2010 and tentatively conjectured that was the most possible; the best upper bound was 1,348. We give a computer-assisted proof that 1,302 is the maximum. | October 20, 2026 |
| [A (243,121,60) difference set in C3 × C9 × C9](results/difference-set-243/) | Whether a (243,121,60) [difference set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_set) exists in $C_3 \times C_9 \times C_9$, open since Vera López and García Sánchez's 1997 table and listed in [Gordon's repository](https://dmgordon.org/difference-sets/). We give a computer-assisted proof that none does. | October 21, 2026 |
| [The three-color Ramsey number of the 10-vertex path](results/path-ramsey/) | The three-color [Ramsey number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey%27s_theorem) $R(P_{10}, P_{10}, P_{10})$ of the [path](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/PathGraph.html) on 10 vertices, conjectured by Faudree and Schelp in 1975 to be 18. It was proved for all long enough paths in 2007 and for paths up to 9 vertices by 2016, leaving 10 as the first open case in [Radziszowski's survey](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/DS1). We give a computer-assisted proof that it is 18. | October 21, 2026 |
