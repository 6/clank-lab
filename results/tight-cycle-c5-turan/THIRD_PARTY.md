# Third-party material and mathematical sources

The construction and lower bound are due to Mubayi and Rödl. The proof uses the partition method and reduction of Bodnár, León, Liu and Pikhurko and the flag-algebra method of Razborov, with the exact versions and statement locators given in the paper. The rational certificates here were constructed for the single forbidden tight five-cycle; they are not BLLP's certificate files for a different forbidden family. No third-party paper or notebook is redistributed in this bundle.

Lean is fetched from leanprover/lean4 at the version in lean/lean-toolchain; it is distributed under Apache-2.0. Mathlib is fetched from leanprover-community/mathlib4 at the revision in lean/lakefile.toml, under Apache-2.0. Their upstream copyright and license notices remain with those dependencies. They are not included in this source bundle.

The independent finite checkers use NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), gmpy2 (LGPL-3.0-or-later), and the GMP libraries (the applicable upstream LGPL/GPL terms). These dependencies retain their upstream licenses and are installed separately. No dependency binary is redistributed. The authored proof sources, new rational certificates, programs, illustration and text are provided under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
