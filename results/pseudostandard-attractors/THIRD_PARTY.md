# Third-party material

No third-party material is included in this folder. The witnesses, the
obstruction, the upper-bound table, the checkers, the figure and the paper
were produced for this result.

The paper quotes the definitions and the conjecture of Ľubomíra Dvořáková
and Veronika Hendrychová, *String attractors of Rote sequences*, Discrete
Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science 26:3 (2024), #2
([doi:10.46298/dmtcs.12385](https://doi.org/10.46298/dmtcs.12385)),
published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.
The quotations are marked and attributed; the paper's examples, recomputed
by the checkers as controls, are cited, not copied. The other cited works
are bibliographic references.
