# Third-party material

The figure ([data/stamps.svg](data/stamps.svg), and [data/stamps.pdf](data/stamps.pdf) as printed in the paper) is derived from public plate scans:

- Harvard plate MA 731, from DASCH (Digital Access to a Sky Century @ Harvard), Harvard College Observatory. DASCH states no license for its images; it asks users to acknowledge it and cite its papers, which the paper does. The figure shows a small filtered region of the scan.
- Heidelberg plates B2247a and B2248b, from the HDAP (Heidelberg Digitized Astronomical Plates) at the GAVO Data Center, released under CC0. This work made use of the HDAP which was produced at Landessternwarte Heidelberg-Königstuhl under grant No. 00.071.2005 of the Klaus-Tschira-Foundation.

No scans, catalogs or ephemerides are redistributed here. The scripts download them from the public services at run time: DASCH, the GAVO Data Center, JPL Horizons, IMCCE SkyBoT, the ESA Gaia archive or VizieR (Gaia DR3), VizieR (APASS DR9) and APPLAUSE. Their terms remain with their providers.

The Python packages listed in [checks/requirements.txt](checks/requirements.txt) are installed separately under their own licenses and are not redistributed.

The paper quotes short passages from the works it cites. All other files use the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
