# Third-party material

The figure ([data/stamps.svg](data/stamps.svg), and [data/stamps.pdf](data/stamps.pdf) as printed in the paper) is derived from public plate scans:

- Harvard plate MA 731, from DASCH (Digital Access to a Sky Century @ Harvard), Harvard College Observatory. DASCH states no license for its images; it asks users to acknowledge it and cite its papers, which the paper does. The figure shows a small filtered region of the scan.
- Heidelberg plates B2247a and B2248b, from the HDAP (Heidelberg Digitized Astronomical Plates) at the GAVO Data Center, released under CC0. This work made use of the HDAP which was produced at Landessternwarte Heidelberg-Königstuhl under grant No. 00.071.2005 of the Klaus-Tschira-Foundation.

The figure [data/ps1-detection.pdf](data/ps1-detection.pdf) (the paper's Figure 2) shows small regions of Pan-STARRS1 DR2 images (skycell 1699.009, two z warps, one warp mask and the z stack) from the Space Telescope Science Institute's MAST. The Pan-STARRS1 Surveys (PS1) and the PS1 public science archive have been made possible through contributions by the Institute for Astronomy, the University of Hawaii, the Pan-STARRS Project Office, the Max-Planck Society and its participating institutes, the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy, Heidelberg and the Max Planck Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics, Garching, The Johns Hopkins University, Durham University, the University of Edinburgh, the Queen's University Belfast, the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics, the Las Cumbres Observatory Global Telescope Network Incorporated, the National Central University of Taiwan, the Space Telescope Science Institute, the National Aeronautics and Space Administration under Grant No. NNX08AR22G issued through the Planetary Science Division of the NASA Science Mission Directorate, the National Science Foundation Grant No. AST-1238877, the University of Maryland, Eotvos Lorand University (ELTE), the Los Alamos National Laboratory, and the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation.

The files in [data/apass/](data/apass/) are cuts of the AAVSO Photometric All-Sky Survey (APASS) DR9 (Henden et al. 2016, VizieR catalog II/336), the stars within 0.75° of each plate's prediction, as `checks/plates.py` downloads them from VizieR, with their SHA-256 hashes in [data/apass/pinned.json](data/apass/pinned.json). The check queries VizieR first and uses them only when VizieR does not answer; when it does, the check confirms that its answer matches them. The AAVSO states no license for APASS; it asks users to acknowledge it: "This research was made possible through the use of the AAVSO Photometric All-Sky Survey (APASS), funded by the Robert Martin Ayers Sciences Fund and NSF AST-1412587." The cuts were retrieved through VizieR (CDS, Strasbourg, France; DOI: 10.26093/cds/vizier).

No scans, ephemerides or other catalogs are redistributed here. The scripts download them from the public services at run time: DASCH, the GAVO Data Center, JPL Horizons, IMCCE SkyBoT, the ESA Gaia archive or VizieR (Gaia DR3), VizieR (APASS DR9, with the pinned cuts above as the fallback), APPLAUSE, and MAST and the PS1 image server (Pan-STARRS1 DR2). Their terms remain with their providers.

The Python packages listed in [checks/requirements.txt](checks/requirements.txt) are installed separately under their own licenses and are not redistributed.

The paper quotes short passages from the works it cites. All other files use the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
