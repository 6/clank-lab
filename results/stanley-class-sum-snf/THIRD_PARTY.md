# Third-party material

No third-party source files are redistributed in this bundle.

The formal proof uses [Lean](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4) and
[Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), both distributed
under the Apache License 2.0. Lake downloads Mathlib and its transitive
dependencies at the revisions recorded in `lean/lake-manifest.json`; those
dependencies retain their own licenses. No dependency source has been modified.

The mathematical sources credited in the paper are used as prior work;
their text and code are not reproduced in the proof files.
