# Third-party material

The Lean proof sources were produced for this result. No source code,
figures or data from the cited mathematical papers are copied into the
Lean project. The formalization uses Mathlib's Bernoulli polynomial,
zeta-value, polynomial, ideal and multivariate polynomial results.

The project fetches [Lean 4](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4), version
4.19.0, and [Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4),
commit `c44e0c8ee63ca166450922a373c7409c5d26b00b`, as dependencies.
Their source trees and compiled caches are not included in this folder.
Lean and Mathlib are distributed upstream under Apache-2.0; their
license and attribution files remain with the fetched dependencies.
Mathlib's pinned transitive dependencies likewise retain their upstream
license notices.
