# Clank Lab

Proposed solutions to open problems in math.

Not yet reviewed by experts. Corrections are welcome as pull requests or issues.

The proofs, formalizations, programs and text were produced by AI models working under the author's direction.

## Theorems

Results that settle a conjecture or question in general.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [Uniform 5-polytopes](results/uniform-5-polytopes/) | Whether every convex [uniform 5-polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_5-polytope) is already known, the case after Conway and Guy's four-dimensional list of 1965. We give a computer-assisted proof that it is. | October 20, 2026 |
| [The EKG sequence](results/ekg-sequence/) | The second-order growth of the [EKG sequence](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/EKGSequence.html), conjectured by Lagarias, Rains and Sloane in 2002. We prove the remaining lower bound, completing the proof, and settle OEIS conjectures that follow. | October 20, 2026 |
| [A planar graph without 4- or 9-cycles](results/steinberg-c4-c9/) | Whether every planar graph with no 4- or 9-cycles is [3-colorable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%B6tzsch%27s_theorem), asked by Lu and coauthors in 2009. We give a computer-assisted proof that one isn't: an 82-vertex counterexample. | October 20, 2026 |
| [Stanley's conjecture on Smith forms of Specht modules](results/stanley-smith-forms/) | Whether the Smith normal form of each [Jucys–Murphy element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jucys%E2%80%93Murphy_element) acting on an integral Specht module is given by products of contents, as conjectured by Richard Stanley in 2016. We give a computer-assisted proof that it is not: the smallest counterexamples have shape (3,1,1). | October 21, 2026 |

## Computed values

Results that settle one specific open case, such as a value a published survey lists as unknown.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [The seventh density Hales–Jewett number](results/density-seven/) | The [density Hales–Jewett number](https://oeis.org/A156989) for words of length 7 over three letters, conjectured by D.H.J. Polymath in 2010 to be 1,302. We give a computer-assisted proof that it is. | October 20, 2026 |
| [A (243,121,60) difference set in C3 × C9 × C9](results/difference-set-243/) | Whether a (243,121,60) [difference set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_set) exists in $C_3 \times C_9 \times C_9$, open since Vera López and García Sánchez's 1997 table and listed in [Gordon's repository](https://dmgordon.org/difference-sets/). We give a computer-assisted proof that none does. | October 21, 2026 |
| [The three-color Ramsey number of the 10-vertex path](results/path-ramsey/) | The three-color [Ramsey number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey%27s_theorem) $R(P_{10}, P_{10}, P_{10})$ of the [path](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/PathGraph.html) on 10 vertices, conjectured by Faudree and Schelp in 1975 to be 18 and listed as the first open case in [Radziszowski's survey](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/DS1). We give a computer-assisted proof that it is. | October 21, 2026 |
