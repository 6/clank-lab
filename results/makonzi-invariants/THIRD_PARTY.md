# Third-party material

The Lean project depends on [Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), licensed under Apache-2.0, and its dependencies, whose licenses remain in their repositories. Lake retrieves these dependencies; they are not redistributed in this folder.

The paper quotes the short statements of Brian Makonzi's Conjecture 5.2 and the defining mathematical formula, with exact version references. No source-paper files or source proof code are redistributed. The Lean proofs in this folder accompany the paper and use the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
