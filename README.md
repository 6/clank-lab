## Results on conjectures and questions

Proofs and counterexamples for published conjectures and questions.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [The EKG sequence](results/ekg-sequence/) | [EKG](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/EKGSequence.html) second-order growth, conjectured in the [2002 PDF abstract](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204011v1) by Lagarias, Rains and Sloane. Haver proved the sharp frontier upper bound. We prove $b(n)=n+n/(3\log n)+o(n/\log n)$ along all sufficiently large indices, smoothing $p,3p$ to $2p$ for odd primes; the original sequence satisfies it at almost all indices. The stated OEIS consequences follow. | October 20, 2026 |
| [Uniform 5-polytopes](results/uniform-5-polytopes/) | Whether the known convex [uniform 5-polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_5-polytope) are all of them. Conway and Guy announced the four-dimensional list in 1965, and [Möller gave a complete proof in 2004](https://ediss.sub.uni-hamburg.de/handle/ediss/689); five was listed as open by Schulte (2017) and Winter (2021). We give a computer-assisted proof that the known list is complete. | October 20, 2026 |
| [A planar graph without 4- or 9-cycles](results/steinberg-c4-c9/) | Whether every planar graph with no 4- or 9-cycles is [3-colorable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_coloring), asked by Lu and coauthors in 2009. We give a computer-assisted proof that one isn't: an 82-vertex counterexample. | October 20, 2026 |
| [Stanley's conjecture on Smith forms of Specht modules](results/stanley-smith-forms/) | Whether the Smith normal form of each [Jucys–Murphy element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jucys%E2%80%93Murphy_element) acting on an integral Specht module is given by products of contents, as conjectured by Richard Stanley in 2016. We disprove it with a written proof, with Lean and computer checks; the smallest counterexamples have shape (3,1,1). | October 21, 2026 |
| [The Smith form of the n-cycle class sum](results/stanley-class-sum-snf/) | The [Smith normal form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_normal_form) of multiplication by the sum of all $n$-cycles in the center of $\mathbb{Z}S_n$, asked by Richard Stanley in [2019](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/335226). We give the invariant factors for every $n$, proving his two conjectured special cases. | October 21, 2026 |
| [Sharp linear bounds for the EKG sequence](results/ekg-bounds/) | Whether the [EKG sequence](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/EKGSequence.html) always satisfies $13n/28 \le a(n) \le 12n/7$, with equality only at $n = 28$ and $n = 7$, as Lagarias, Rains and Sloane conjectured in 2002; they proved $n/260 \le a(n) \le 14n$. We give a computer-assisted proof of the conjecture. | October 22, 2026 |
| [Existence of locally toroidal regular 4-polytopes](results/locally-toroidal-polytopes/) | [Schulte–Weiss Problem 16 (2006)](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608397) asks to classify these [regular polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_regular_polytope) for distinct odd parameters. Only $(3,5)$ and its dual were previously known. We prove the existence part, conjectured by McMullen–Schulte in 2002; finiteness of the universal polytopes and group classification remain open. | October 22, 2026 |
| [Locked tromino tilings of rectangles](results/locked-trominoes/) | Which rectangles have a locked [tromino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyomino) tiling, in which no two tiles can be re-cut into two other trominoes. Tucker-Foltz (2023) excluded sides of at most 3, built them for $3 \mid m$, $m \ge 6$, $n \ge 100$, and conjectured that $n \ge 4$ suffices. We prove the conjecture, completing the classification. | October 22, 2026 |
| [String attractors of generalized pseudostandard sequences](results/pseudostandard-attractors/) | Whether every pseudopalindromic prefix of a binary generalized pseudostandard sequence has a [string attractor](https://doi.org/10.1145/3188745.3188814) of at most four positions, as conjectured by Dvořáková and Hendrychová in [August 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00850v1); smaller bounds were known for three subclasses. We give a computer-assisted proof that it fails at length 36. | October 22, 2026 |
| [Rank-two scattering coefficients](results/scattering-coefficients/) | Whether the coefficients $\tau^{1,5}(2j,j)$ of a rank-two [cluster scattering diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluster_algebra) are given by a binomial sum, as Elgin, Reading and Stella conjectured in 2024. [Burcroff, Lee, Mou, Musiker and Reineke](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14672) proved most of their conjectures in 2025 and listed this one as unsolved. We prove it for every $j \ge 1$, in the convention of the conjecture's table and code. | October 22, 2026 |
| [A monotone function with different query and partition costs](results/monotone-query-gap/) | Whether average query and subcube partition costs agree for every monotone [Boolean function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_function) at every coin bias, asked by Köhler-Schindler and Steif in [2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12995). Their nonmonotone ALL-EQUAL and four-input $h$ examples have strict gaps (arXiv v1, Example 2.4 and Section 2.3). We answer no with an explicit five-input example at fair coins. | October 22, 2026 |
| [Fathi's consecutive-integers bound does not extend to multitwists](results/multitwist-exceptions/) | Whether Fathi's consecutive-exponent bound extends to multitwists, asked by [Ivanov in 2006](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608325), after Fathi's single-twist theorem. On the five-holed sphere we give a written counterexample with a fixed positive two-component multitwist and varying pseudo-Anosov bases, whose exceptional exponents are arbitrarily far apart. The weaker count question remains open. | October 22, 2026 |
| [A counterexample to a lit-only sigma-game conjecture](results/lit-only-sigma/) | Whether a connected graph's same-start light-number gap exceeding half its order forces a complete multipartite graph, as [Goldwasser, Wang and Wu conjectured in 2011](https://doi.org/10.37236/701), motivated by their multipartite examples. We give computer-assisted counterexamples on $7m$ vertices for every $m\geq1$, with same-start gap at least $4m$. | October 23, 2026 |
| [Strongly cospectral sets in cubelike graphs](results/cubelike-cospectrality/) | Whether Árnadóttir and Godsil's bound for strongly cospectral sets in [cubelike graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayley_graph) is tight in every dimension ([2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.07568)); they attained it in dimensions three through six. We prove that the seven-dimensional maximum is four, rather than their bound of eight, answering no. | October 23, 2026 |
| [Greedy play can require more colors in the coloring game](results/game-grundy-gap/) | Whether the game Grundy number is always at most the [game chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_chromatic_number), asked by Havet and Zhu in [June 2011](https://inria.hal.science/inria-00600738v1/document) and left open by Hollom (2023). The same counterexample now has the exact values $\chi_g(G)=5$ and $\Gamma_g(G)=6$. Arbitrarily large gaps remain open. | October 23, 2026 |
| [The weak parking-space conjecture](results/weak-parking/) | Armstrong, Reiner and Rhoades's [2012 conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.1760) asks whether two parking-space representations of a finite [reflection group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflection_group), including their cyclic symmetry, agree. Every irreducible real type except $E_7$ and $E_8$ was known. We give a computer-assisted proof for these last two types, completing the weak conjecture. | October 23, 2026 |
| [The strong Lefschetz property for complex reflection coinvariants](results/coinvariant-lefschetz/) | Whether every irreducible [complex reflection group's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_reflection_group) coinvariant ring has the strong Lefschetz property, asked by Wachi in [2013](https://doi.org/10.34508/repsympo.2013.0_140). Previous results covered every type except $G_{34}$. We give a computer-assisted proof of that remaining case, completing the general assertion over $\mathbb{C}$. | October 23, 2026 |
| [Symmetric chains in noncrossing partition lattices](results/noncrossing-symmetric-chains/) | Whether every well-generated complex reflection group's [noncrossing partition lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncrossing_partition) has a symmetric chain decomposition, asked by [Mühle in 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06942v1). Earlier results covered the infinite families and most exceptional types. We give a computer-assisted proof for the remaining types, completing the chain assertion. We do not settle the stronger Boolean question. | October 23, 2026 |

## Results on individual cases

Each addresses one specific case of a problem that remains open in general.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [The seventh density Hales–Jewett number](results/density-seven/) | The largest set of seven-letter words over three letters with no combinatorial line (the seventh [density Hales–Jewett number](https://oeis.org/A156989)). D.H.J. Polymath knew the values through six and the 1,302 lower bound by [March 2009](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2009/03/04/dhj3-900-999-density-hales-jewett-type-numbers/), later tentatively conjecturing equality in [their 2010 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.0374). We give a computer-assisted proof that it is. | October 20, 2026 |
| [A (243,121,60) difference set in C3 × C9 × C9](results/difference-set-243/) | Whether a (243,121,60) [difference set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_set) exists in $C_3 \times C_9 \times C_9$, recorded as unresolved in Vera López and García Sánchez's 1997 table and listed in [Gordon's repository](https://dmgordon.org/difference-sets/). We give a computer-assisted proof that none does. | October 21, 2026 |
| [The three-color Ramsey number of the 10-vertex path](results/path-ramsey/) | The three-color [Ramsey number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey%27s_theorem) $R(P_{10}, P_{10}, P_{10})$ of the [path](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/PathGraph.html) on 10 vertices, suggested by Faudree–Schelp (1975) to be 18 and [stated as a conjecture by Dybizbański–Dzido–Radziszowski (2012)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5851). Paths up to 9 vertices were settled by 2016, leaving 10 as the first open case in [Radziszowski's survey](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/DS1). We give a computer-assisted proof that it is 18. | October 21, 2026 |
| [The Thomson problem for N = 10](results/thomson-10/) | The lowest-energy arrangement of ten charges on a sphere, a case of the [Thomson problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomson_problem) (1904). We prove that the bicapped square antiprism minimizes Coulomb energy among all ten distinct unit-sphere points, with equality exactly up to orthogonal maps and relabeling. | October 22, 2026 |
| [Nonexistence of Williamson matrices of order 65](results/williamson-order-65/) | Whether [Williamson matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williamson_conjecture) exist at order 65, described by [Bright in 2019](https://cs.curtisbright.com/extended-research-statement.pdf) as “currently the smallest order for which it is unknown” whether they exist; 67 remains open. A computer-assisted search finds none. The CPU search is replayable, with receipts and two coverage and accounting checkers. | October 22, 2026 |
