# Checking details

## Sources used during development

Biane reproduces the character-sum formula, equation (4) of Goupil and Schaeffer, as equations (1)–(2), and gives a short algebraic proof of the positive-term version. This paper uses the character-sum form, derived in Proposition 2.1 from Frobenius’s formula. The main README retains both credits and their distinct roles.

The DOI archives v4; v5 is a prepared correction with no new DOI yet.

## Further credits

- Richard P. Stanley, [Smith normal form of conjugacy class actions](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/335226) — poses the question and two conjectures with motivating examples.
- Nathan Lindzey, [Smith Normal Form of a Cayley Graph of the Symmetric Group](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/334899) — inspires Stanley’s question.
- Bruce E. Sagan, [The Symmetric Group: Representations, Combinatorial Algorithms, and Symmetric Functions](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4757-6804-6) — presents Frobenius’s class-multiplication formula and classical hook characters used as the starting point.
- Alain Goupil and Gilles Schaeffer, [Factoring n-cycles and counting maps of given genus](https://doi.org/10.1006/eujc.1998.0215) — gives the long-cycle connection coefficients.
- Philippe Biane, [Nombre de factorisations d’un grand cycle](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/s51biane.pdf) — reproduces the character-sum formula and proves the positive-term form.
- Darij Grinberg, [An introduction to the symmetric group algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20706) — supplies a contemporary exposition of class sums and representations.
- Tommy Wuxing Cai and Richard P. Stanley, [The Smith Normal Form of a Matrix Associated with Young’s Lattice](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00922) — provides related context.
- Lean contributors, [Lean 4](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4) — supplies the proof assistant.
- mathlib contributors, [mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4) — supplies the formal-proof library.
