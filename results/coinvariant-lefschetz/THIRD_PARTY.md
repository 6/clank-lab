# Third-party material

The paper, input tables, certificates, and checker sources in this folder are distributed under Apache-2.0. No third-party source code, executables, or article files are included. Mathematical constructions and results are credited in the paper; Wachi's programs and the programs accompanying the cited papers are not copied into this bundle.

The checks require separately installed Python, SymPy, a C++ compiler with OpenMP, FLINT, GMP, Singular, and GNU time. Each dependency remains under its upstream license. The Python requirement is recorded in `checks/requirements.txt`; the other requirements and installation commands are in the README. Invoking an independently installed program does not relicense that program.
