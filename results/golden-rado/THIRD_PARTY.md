# Third-party material

The Lean proof sources were produced for this result. No source code,
figures or data from the cited papers are copied into this project; the
definitions follow Hopkins's Definition 2, restated in Lean.

The project fetches [Lean 4](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4), version
4.19.0, and [Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4),
commit `c44e0c8ee63ca166450922a373c7409c5d26b00b`, as dependencies.
Their source trees and compiled caches are not included in this folder.
Lean and Mathlib are distributed upstream under Apache-2.0; their license
and attribution files remain with the fetched dependencies. Mathlib's
pinned transitive dependencies retain their upstream license notices.
