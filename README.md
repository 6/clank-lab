# Clank Lab

Proposed solutions to open problems in math.

Not yet reviewed by experts. Corrections are welcome as pull requests or issues.

The proofs, formalizations, programs and text were produced by AI models (from OpenAI and Anthropic), working under the author's direction.

## Theorems

Results that settle a stated conjecture or question in general.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [ekg-sequence](results/ekg-sequence/) | The second-order growth of the [EKG sequence](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/EKGSequence.html), conjectured by Lagarias, Rains and Sloane in 2002. We prove the remaining lower bound, completing the proof, and settle several OEIS conjectures that follow. | October 20, 2026 |
| [uniform-5-polytopes](results/uniform-5-polytopes/) | Whether every convex [uniform polytope in five dimensions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_5-polytope) is already known, the next case after Conway and Guy's four-dimensional list of 1965. We give a computer-assisted proof that it is. | October 20, 2026 |
| [steinberg-c4-c9](results/steinberg-c4-c9/) | Whether every planar graph with no cycles of length 4 or 9 can be [3-colored](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%B6tzsch%27s_theorem), asked by Lu, Wang, Wang, Bu, Montassier and Raspaud in 2009. We give a computer-assisted proof that one can't: an 82-vertex counterexample. | October 20, 2026 |

## Computed values

Results that determine one value a survey or table lists as open.

| Result | Open question | Published |
|---|---|---|
| [density-seven](results/density-seven/) | The [density Hales–Jewett number](https://oeis.org/A156989) for words of length 7 over three letters, conjectured by D.H.J. Polymath in 2010 to be 1,302. We give a computer-assisted proof that it is 1,302. | October 20, 2026 |
