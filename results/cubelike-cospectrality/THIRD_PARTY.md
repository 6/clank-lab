No third-party source files or figures are redistributed in this bundle.

The proof uses [Lean](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4) and
[Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), both distributed
under the Apache License 2.0. Lake downloads Mathlib and its transitive
dependencies at the revisions recorded in `lean/lake-manifest.json`;
those dependencies retain their own licenses. No dependency source has
been modified.

The mathematical constructions, character criterion and prior computations
credited in the paper are used as prior work. The programs, formalization,
and figure in this bundle are new implementations; the earlier authors'
code and figures are not copied.
