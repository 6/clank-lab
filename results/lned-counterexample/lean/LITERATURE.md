# Bibliography and reading depth

October 25, 2026. Statements below distinguish readable preprints,
published abstracts, reviews, and inaccessible bodies. A scope read checks
definitions and stated hypotheses; it is not independent verification of
every proof. The table makes no first-publication or exhaustive-priority claim.
DOIs identify final articles; an arXiv read does not establish identity of
every statement with its journal version.
