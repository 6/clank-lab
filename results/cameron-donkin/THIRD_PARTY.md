# Third-party material

The Lean project depends on [mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), licensed under Apache-2.0, and [Lean](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4), also licensed under Apache-2.0. Lake downloads the pinned dependencies; they are not copied into this folder. Their own license and attribution files accompany those downloads.

No third-party paper text, OEIS data table or repository source is reproduced. The paper and README cite the mathematical work they use. The original proof sources and text in this folder are covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
