# Third-party material

The paper includes a brief attributed quotation of Conjecture 5 from John
Goldwasser, Xinmao Wang and Yaokun Wu, *Minimum light numbers in the
σ-game and lit-only σ-game on unicyclic and grid graphs*, Electronic
Journal of Combinatorics 18(1) (2011), P214
([doi:10.37236/701](https://doi.org/10.37236/701)). The article's page and
PDF state no explicit reuse license; the quotation is separately
attributed and is not presented as Apache-2.0 material.

No third-party code, data or figures are included. The witness,
certificate, Lean formalization, checking programs, figures and remaining
text were produced for this result. Other cited works are bibliographic
references. The Lean project fetches Lean and Mathlib, both under
Apache-2.0, as dependencies; their source trees are not included.
