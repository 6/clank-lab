# Checking notes

## Sources used during development

Wenhua Zhao's [Images of ideals under derivations and E-derivations of univariate polynomial algebras over a field of characteristic zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.06125v1) supplies the membership criterion used here: Lemma 4.5 and Corollary 4.6. Examples 2.4 and 3.6 there already give related parity obstructions for other operators. The contribution here is the explicit half-step specialization and the resulting refutation.
